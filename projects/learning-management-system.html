Learning Management System

A learning-management platform for managing courses, students and academic records in one centralized system.

Role
Design & development
Type
Web application
Platform

Overview

Educational information is often spread across separate files and tools, which makes it hard to manage and access. The Learning Management System brings courses, students and academic records together in one central platform.

It organizes course content, student enrollment and progress, with interactive learning modules, improving access to academic data for everyone who needs it.

Key features

  • Course management

    Create and organize courses and their learning modules.

  • Student enrollment

    Enroll students in courses and keep their details in one place.

  • Progress tracking

    Follow how students are progressing through their courses.

  • Academic records

    Keep academic records centralized and easy to access.

What I did

  • Planned how courses, students and academic records relate to each other.
  • Built the web interface with HTML, CSS and JavaScript.
  • Used Python to handle the data and records behind the platform.
